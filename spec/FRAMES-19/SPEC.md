# FRAMES-19: The resolved document

Status: accepted

## Summary

The resolved document, frames' own lockfile: what resolve has already done, its shape, and the rules for reading it.

## Motivation

An edit opened on another machine, or on the same machine a month later, should come out the same. In an NLE it often does not: a font is missing or a different version and titles reflow, a pack or a LUT has changed, a tracker run again lands a little elsewhere, a template's defaults moved. What can differ between machines and over time (installed fonts and packs, what a provider shaped, theme and preset values, template arguments) must be fixed once, or a result cannot be made again. Writing every such decision out as values, with every file the roles still open pinned by hash, makes the resolved document enough to make the result again, and gives evaluation one input with nothing left to look up.

## Rules

The resolved document is frames' own lockfile: what `resolve` writes, and what evaluation and `render` read. It is not an exchange format, and frames reads only what resolve wrote. It is JSON with `"kind": "sequence"`, the one document kind ([FRAMES-6](../FRAMES-6/SPEC.md)), and `"resolved"` set to the version of the frames that wrote it ([FRAMES-18](../FRAMES-18/SPEC.md)).

What resolve has already done:

- **Keys are checked.** A resolved document carries only the keys below.
- **Areas and points.** Every area is `[x, y, w, h]` and every point `[x, y]` ([FRAMES-2](../FRAMES-2/SPEC.md)), except a clip area, whose value changes with the clip it names: it stays `{clip, padding}`, its `padding` a theme length with the length scale applied ([FRAMES-2](../FRAMES-2/SPEC.md)), and a point of one stays `{area, point}`. Resolve has checked that its id names a clip it may name, one with the same parent or its own parent, and that no cycle holds. Each stays in the coordinate space of the clip that wrote it ([FRAMES-10](../FRAMES-10/SPEC.md)): a crop in the media pixels of its media, a parented clip's `position`, geometry and masks in the space its parent gives, and an unparented clip's in output pixels.
- **Files are pinned.** Every file a role opens (media, mattes, LUTs, displacement maps, impulse responses, fonts) is written as its absolute path and its SHA-256. An image sequence is pinned as its ordered list of files, each with its SHA-256, so a frame added, removed or changed is refused. A page of a PDF is pinned as its file with the number of its page ([FRAMES-3](../FRAMES-3/SPEC.md#pdf-pages)). A command that opens what a resolved document pins (`render`, `still`, `sheet`, `extract` and `loudness`) checks every hash before any role starts and refuses a file whose hash differs.
- **Decisions are written down.** Conformed crops, the measured size of a content-fit frame ([FRAMES-6](../FRAMES-6/SPEC.md)), parents, track mattes and tracks are values, not rules to apply again. A track is the target's quadrilateral on each frame followed and the similarity motion fitted to it ([FRAMES-12](../FRAMES-12/SPEC.md)). A frame's homography is not a decision but the closed form of the target's quadrilateral and the frame's ([FRAMES-27](../FRAMES-27/SPEC.md#the-homography)), so it is not written, and evaluation computes it.
- **Parents are named.** A clip's `parent` is the `id` of its parent clip in the same document, or `null`. Resolve has checked it: it names a clip with a picture in the same document, and no parent chain holds a cycle ([FRAMES-10](../FRAMES-10/SPEC.md)).
- **Anchor points are applied.** A clip with a box of its own (a title, a media clip, a storyline picture, a rect, an ellipse or a null placed by `width` and `height`) writes `size`, its box in output pixels (a shape's box as written, without its `outset`), `anchor`, the point its scale and rotation act around in the box's own pixels (origin at the box's top left), and `position`, where that point goes in the clip's coordinate space. A clip placed by `placement` is written with the `position` its placement gives. A storyline picture's box is the one [FRAMES-9](../FRAMES-9/SPEC.md) gives (the frame, or under content fit its crop's pixel size), which its conformed crop fills. A shape or a null placed by `area` or `points` and an adjustment have no box: their `size` is `null`, and their `anchor` is `null` unless the clip names one, so their scale and rotation act around the centre of their geometry's box at each time ([FRAMES-1](../FRAMES-1/SPEC.md)). A shape that names an anchor writes it as the point it names of its geometry's box, or, where its `area` or `points` hold a clip area, whose box changes with the clip it names, as the placement name, and evaluation takes that point of the geometry's box at each time. `position` is `null` unless the clip writes one, and then it is where the anchor point goes. A clip with a corner pin writes `size`, its box as a clip with a box of its own does, whose corners its corner pin places, and its `position`, `anchor`, `scale` and `rotation` as `null` ([FRAMES-27](../FRAMES-27/SPEC.md)).
- **Clips are named.** Every clip keeps its place in the document and its `id`, so `evaluate` and `locate` can name what made each thing drawn.
- **No names.** Theme colours, text styles, shape styles, presets and template arguments are written out as values, and so are the theme colours a preset names (a cover or a bloom `color`) in the theme where the preset is chosen. A preset is written with its arguments applied ([FRAMES-14](../FRAMES-14/SPEC.md)): it holds plain values, and no `params` and no `{"param"}` remain. A stroke's `start` and `end` are `null` where the end has no head, or its head preset's `points`, `length` and `inset` beside the end's `scale` as a keyframe list, `{points, length, inset, scale}`, each vertex written whole as `{vertex, inTangent, outTangent}` ([FRAMES-11](../FRAMES-11/SPEC.md#shapes)). Theme lengths are already multiplied by their length scale ([FRAMES-1](../FRAMES-1/SPEC.md)). Colours are `color(srgb r g b / a)`, the encoded working value at full precision ([FRAMES-26](../FRAMES-26/SPEC.md)).
- **Text is shaped.** Every title carries its box size (at the static tracking, line height and background padding), its text box `[x, y, w, h]` in that box at the same values, so a background `padding` of `[8, 12]` puts the text box 12 right and 8 down from the box's top left in a box 24 wider and 16 taller than it ([FRAMES-11](../FRAMES-11/SPEC.md)), and in a vertical writing mode 8 right and 12 down in a box 16 wider and 24 taller ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#writing-mode)), its `width` (the width it wraps at, `null` for a title without one), its background (`null`, or a timed `{color, padding, radius}`), its `writingMode` (`horizontal-tb`, `vertical-rl` or `vertical-lr`, its default resolved, [FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#writing-mode)), its `direction` (`ltr` or `rtl`, the paragraph direction its text was laid out in, `auto` resolved, [FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#direction)), its `align` (`left`, `center` or `right`, its default resolved), its `lineHeight` (a keyframe list), its `text`, its `words`, its `lines`, its runs in order, line by line and each line's in the order its glyphs are placed ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#direction)), and its `units`. `text` is the title's text, the text of its runs joined in order with its arguments applied and its line endings read as `\n` ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#runs)), and every range is `[start, end)` in Unicode scalars over it ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#units)). `words` is the title's words in reading order, each its range, a word a line breaks inside one range ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#units)). `lines` is its lines in order, written for every title, each `{width, baseline, ascent, descent, range, clusters, hanging}`: `width` the line's width at the static tracking, `baseline` the y of its baseline in the title's box at the static line height, its x in a vertical writing mode, whose words are read on its axes as [FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#writing-mode) reads them, so `width` is the line's length down the line there, `ascent` and `descent` the largest ascent and descent among its runs ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#lines)), `range` its range, and `clusters` its characters in order, each `[run, first, count, start, end, advance, level, cursive]`: its glyphs as a range, `count` glyphs from glyph `first` of the run at index `run` and on into the runs after it, its range `[start, end)`, `advance` the sum of its glyphs' advances, which holds no tracking ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#spacing)), or for the first character of a horizontal-in-vertical composition the composition's advance, 1 em of its run's size ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#horizontal-in-vertical-composition)), `level` the embedding level it lies at on its line, after rule L1 of UAX #9 ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#direction)), and `cursive` the cursive scripts it is a letter of where tracking is placed ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#spacing)), none for a letter set upright in a vertical line, their ISO 15924 codes (the short Script values of Unicode, such as `Arab`) separated by single spaces as ScriptExtensions.txt writes them, none twice, and `""` for a character that is a letter of none ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#spacing)). Two characters that share a code in `cursive` take no tracking between them, which needs no Unicode data. Clusters are written in logical order, and the order they are placed in, their visual order, is the one rule L2 gives from their levels, which needs no Unicode data. Its pen start, where its glyph box begins ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#units)), is its first glyph's pen x, its pen y in a vertical writing mode. A character's own glyphs lie in one run, since no grapheme cluster is split over runs or faces ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#runs)), but a group of glyphs ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#units)) is one group whatever faces set them, and one set in several, as a horizontal-in-vertical composition one of whose characters falls back to another face is, lies in a run of each face, side by side in the order its glyphs are placed: its range starts in the first of them and runs on into the next. Every rule that takes a cluster's glyphs, evaluation's moves, glyph boxes and units and their drawing, takes this range, and one that ends in its own run names that run's glyphs alone. A character with no glyph of its own, a character of a group of glyphs other than its first ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#units)), is a range of count 0 that starts after its group's last glyph, in the run that glyph lies in, with `advance` 0, and lies where its group's first character lies. A line on which no character has a glyph, which only default ignorable scalars that no face draws give ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#faces)), has no group: each of its characters is a range of count 0 at glyph 0 of the line's first run, with `advance` 0, lying at the line's start with a glyph box of no width, and that run, in the style of its first character's run, is written for the line even when it holds no glyph. `hanging` is the spaces that hang past the line's end ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#lines)), in order and in the form of `clusters`, each at the paragraph embedding level, which are none of its characters and lie in no range of it, so a line with no character has no cluster and all its spaces in `hanging`. Resolve has split the text into words, characters and units once, with the Unicode data it read ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#units)), so evaluation splits none again and assigns no character or glyph to a unit. `units` is `null`, or, for a title animated by unit ([FRAMES-14](../FRAMES-14/SPEC.md)), its units in reading order, empty for a title with no character ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#units)), each `{box, glyphBox, characters, hangingLines, parts}` with `box` the unit's box and `glyphBox` its glyph box ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md)), each `[x, y, w, h]` in the title's box, `characters` the characters that go with the unit, those it holds and the spaces that go with it, as `[line, first, count]`, the line's index, the index of the first of them among its line's clusters and their count, since they are one stretch of one line ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#units)), `hangingLines` the indices of the lines whose `hanging` it draws, in order, the unit's glyphs being those of every character of that stretch and of every space in the `hanging` of those lines ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#units)), and `parts` the parts of highlight and underline pieces it carries as `[run, piece, x0, x1]`: run index, the piece's index among the run's pieces, and the part's span across the line in the title's box, in y in a vertical writing mode ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md)), a run's highlight piece and its underline piece at one index sharing the part, a unit carrying one part of a piece or several where its characters lie apart in it ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#units)), and the leftmost part of a piece starting at the piece's own left end and the rightmost ending at its own right end, which evaluation reaches past to the piece's ends as drawn, its padding included. A run carries `source`, the index of the title's run it is set from (a title's run set over several lines or in several faces is several runs, and a piece's characters are the characters of its line's runs of that `source`), its font file, face, hash, size, its colour as a keyframe list, its stroke (`null`, or a timed `{width, color}`), its `baselineShift` as a keyframe list, `sideways`, `true` where its glyphs are set sideways in a vertical line and are drawn turned 90 degrees clockwise and `false` otherwise, `horizontalScale`, the scale its glyphs are drawn at along their own horizontal axis, below 1 where they are the glyphs of a horizontal-in-vertical composition narrowed to its square and 1 otherwise, a glyph run holding glyphs of one orientation and one horizontal scale ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#writing-mode), [FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#horizontal-in-vertical-composition)), its `staticTracking`, the tracking resolve set between two neighbouring characters with glyphs of their own of which one of its characters comes first in the text, in output pixels, 0 when it has none ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#spacing)), its `tracking` (`null`, or a keyframe list of the tracking added there beyond its static tracking, in output pixels), its highlight (`{color, padding, radius, rects}` or `null`) and underline (`{color, width, offset, rects}` or `null`), each leaf there but `rects` a keyframe list, and `rects` the run's pieces in line order, those of one line in the text order of their first characters, in the title's box: a highlight piece as `[x, y, w, h]`, from the run's ascent to its descent without padding, and an underline piece as `[x, y, w]`, from x and w long on the run's alphabetic baseline at y, or in a vertical writing mode from y and w long down the run's alphabetic baseline at x ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#writing-mode)). A glyph is `[id, x, y, dx, dy]`: its glyph id, its pen position (x, y) in the title's box, with kerning, features, the static tracking and the static line height applied and the baseline shift not, and its placement offset (dx, dy) from the pen to its origin ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#spacing)), and it is drawn from its origin at (x + dx, y + dy), narrowed about it along its own horizontal axis by its run's `horizontalScale` and turned 90 degrees clockwise about it in a `sideways` run ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#writing-mode)). So evaluation raises the glyphs and the pieces by `baselineShift` at each instant, moves them by `tracking` along their lines, and moves them with their lines by `lineHeight`, on the axes of the title's writing mode. A title written as a string is one run in the clip's style, and a run's character attributes come from its own style with the title's `character`, its static value with the structure its keyframes give, merged over it, and its keyframes timing the leaves of the merged value ([FRAMES-11](../FRAMES-11/SPEC.md)).
- **Masks and mattes are geometry and ids.** A clip's `mask` is `null` or a timed list of masks in order, each `{geometry, feather, invert, mode}` with the numbers of its geometry and its `feather` keyframe lists: `geometry` an object whose one key, `rect`, `ellipse` or `path`, names its form and holds the keys the display list writes beside that `kind` ([FRAMES-20](../FRAMES-20/SPEC.md#paths)), in the clip's own coordinate space (the media pixels of its media for a picture, the space its geometry is in for any other clip), a path mask a closed `path`, and `feather` in output pixels. Each number a keyframe moves on its own is its own keyframe list: a rect's or an ellipse's `rect` and a rect's `radius`, each of a path's `vertices`, `inTangents` and `outTangents`, its tangents `[0, 0]` where none is written, and for a rect or an ellipse given by `width`, `height` and `position`, those three in place of `rect` (`{"rect": {"width": [...], "height": [...], "position": [...], "radius": [...]}}`), since keyframes time each of them apart ([FRAMES-4](../FRAMES-4/SPEC.md)). A clip's `trackMatte` is `null` or `{clip, mode, invert}`, `clip` the `id` of the matte clip. The matte clip keeps its own entry, and evaluation draws it only as a matte.
- **One clock.** Every instant on a sequence is sequence time, and keyframes keep their eases, a spring as `{"kind": "spring", "bounce": b}` with `b` a number ([FRAMES-4](../FRAMES-4/SPEC.md)). A clip's `in` and `out` stay on its clip time and its `speed` as written ([FRAMES-8](../FRAMES-8/SPEC.md)). A clip with `timeRemap` carries it as a keyframe list in sequence time whose values are media seconds, and its `speed` is 1. A clip without one has `timeRemap` `null`, and its clip time is its media time.
- **Lanes kept.** Clips keep document order and carry their signed `lane`, so evaluation stacks them by [FRAMES-9](../FRAMES-9/SPEC.md). A storyline transition keeps its `over`, which evaluation applies at each time.

How it is written, so the same plan gives the same bytes:

- **One layout.** It is UTF-8 JSON indented by two spaces, one member or element to a line, except that a list of numbers, strings, booleans or `null` stays on one line, and it ends with a line break. Keys come in one fixed order for each kind of object, `kind` and `"resolved"` first, never sorted.
- **One number form.** A number is the shortest decimal that reads back to the same 64-bit floating point value, with no exponent, no fraction when it is whole (`1080`, not `1080.0`), and `0` for negative zero. A string escapes `"`, `\` and control characters only, and writes every other character as itself.
- **Hashes.** A SHA-256 is 64 lower case hexadecimal digits under `sha256`, next to the absolute `file` it pins. Pinned media names what it is under `kind`, as a media object of the document does ([FRAMES-3](../FRAMES-3/SPEC.md)), with its own keys beside it. A video, image or sound file is `{"kind": "file", file, sha256, frames}`, `frames` the frame times of a video file and `null` for an image or a sound file. An image sequence is `{"kind": "imageSequence", files, frames}`, `files` its frame files in order, each `{file, sha256}`, and `frames` the media time of each. A page of a PDF is `{"kind": "page", file, sha256, page}`, `page` its number from 1, written for every PDF, so a PDF of one page named as a file is written with `page` 1 and the form does not depend on how the document named it. A nested sequence in a `media` is a resolved document, of kind `sequence`, so a reader tells pinned media and a compound clip apart by `kind` alone.
- **Timed values.** A timed leaf ([FRAMES-4](../FRAMES-4/SPEC.md)) is a keyframe list, a list of `{at, value, ease}` in time order, where `ease` shapes the span that starts at its keyframe, and a keyframable value is a **timed value**: its structure with each timed leaf in it a keyframe list and each static leaf its value. So `opacity` and `position` are keyframe lists, a stroke is `{paint, width, join, cap, ...}` with its `width` and a colour `paint` keyframe lists and its `align`, `join` and `cap` names, and a filter is a list of entries `{ops, mix}`, each the ops of one entry of the choice (its preset's, or the one op written in place), each op named by its `kind` as a preset's `ops` names it, with every op parameter that is a number or a colour a keyframe list, a parameter a preset argument fills taking that argument's keyframes, a list parameter whose elements arguments fill (`levels` `input` and `output`) a list of one keyframe list for each element, so each element keeps the keyframes and eases of its argument, and its `mix` a keyframe list. A leaf written without keyframes is one keyframe at its clip's instant ([FRAMES-8](../FRAMES-8/SPEC.md#a-clips-instant)), and one not written is its plain value there, the layers below merged, `null` leaves written as the merged value and theme colours and `true` written out. The exception is a tracked clip's followed geometry ([FRAMES-12](../FRAMES-12/SPEC.md#the-track)), its `crop` and `mask` on its own media, or its `position`, `area`, `points`, `cornerPin` and a shape's or an adjustment's `mask` on its parent's media: a leaf of it written without keyframes is true on the track's frame and is written as its value in place of a keyframe list, and evaluation does not bring it back. A keyframe list is a list of objects that hold `at`, and no value of such a leaf is one, so the two are read apart. A `scale` value is `[sx, sy]`, a number written as `[s, s]`. A corner `radius` value, a mask's, a background's and a highlight's included, is `[topLeft, topRight, bottomRight, bottomLeft]`, a number written as `[r, r, r, r]`, and a value read there that is one number is that radius at every corner ([FRAMES-11](../FRAMES-11/SPEC.md#shapes)). A keyframable value that is off (a `fill` or `stroke` of `false`, a `shadow` or `border` of `false`, no filter) is `null`.
- **Every key written.** An entry carries every key of its kind, `null` where it has none. Only the outermost document carries `"resolved"`, and a nested one is the same shape without it. Track frames are frames of media, so they keep media time, as `frames` does. An audio clip is drawn by nothing, so it is an audio entry only, whatever its media is ([FRAMES-13](../FRAMES-13/SPEC.md#audio-clips)).

### Reading a resolved document

- Resolve is deterministic for the same document, arguments, media, theme, presets, packs, fonts and tracks.
- Evaluating and rendering a resolved document needs its pinned files and nothing else. It names no role: the command that renders it picks them ([FRAMES-21](../FRAMES-21/SPEC.md)), and the same result is promised for the same ones.
- A frames reads a resolved document written by a frames of its own major version at its minor version or an earlier one, and refuses any other, naming both versions. Made again by the version that wrote it, a resolved document gives the same result. A later version gives the same result too, except where a patch brought evaluation or a role back to this specification.
- Display lists and the mix plan have a JSON form too, with `"displayList"` and `"mixPlan"` set to the version of the frames that wrote them, so what evaluation gives a role can be checked, recorded and replayed apart from it. They are read by the same rule as a resolved document.
- **A line's pen positions are checked.** On a line, a cluster's pen start is its first glyph's pen x, and the line's clusters followed by its `hanging` are taken in visual order, the order rule L2 gives from their levels ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#direction)), those with no glyph of their own left out. Each one starts at the pen start of the one before it plus that one's `advance`, along the line's axis ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#writing-mode)), plus, when both are characters that share no code in `cursive`, the `staticTracking` of the run of whichever of the two comes first in the text, since tracking lies between neighbouring characters but two letters of one cursive script and none lies at a line's ends or beside a space that hangs, so a ligature that ends a line takes none after it ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#spacing)). The rightmost character taken ends at the leftmost one's pen start plus the line's `width`, and a line with no character taken, one that holds nothing but spaces or whose characters have no glyph, has a `width` of 0. A resolved document whose numbers break this by more than a millionth of a pixel stops when it is read, naming the title's place, since a document whose advances held its tracking, or whose tracking held part of its advances, would draw the same glyphs and report other characters' boxes ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#spacing)).
- **A title's ranges are checked.** Every range of a title, a cluster's, a line's and a word's, lies within the scalars of its `text`, a cluster's holds at least one scalar, and a line's clusters followed by its `hanging` go up along the text, each starting at or after the end of the one before it. A resolved document that breaks this stops when it is read, naming the place of the range or the title, since evaluation reads a title's text over its ranges.
- **A title's indices are checked.** Every index of a title names something the title holds: a cluster's run is one of its runs, its `first` one of that run's glyphs, or that run's end for a range of count 0, and its glyphs lie in that run and the runs after it, a unit's `characters` lie in its line's `clusters`, each of its `hangingLines` is one of the title's lines, and a part's run is one of the title's runs with a highlight or an underline and its piece one of that run's pieces in each of the two the run has, so no part names a run with neither. A glyph's id is one an OpenType face can hold, 0 to 65535. A resolved document that breaks this stops when it is read, naming the place of the index, since evaluation and drawing take what each index names.
- Evaluation and `render` trust a resolved document as they trust resolve. They check its version and the ties between its numbers written above (a track's `motion`, a line's pen positions, a title's ranges and indices), and the commands that open its files its hashes, not its rules again. Evaluation opens no file.

## Alternatives considered

An exchange format (FCPXML, OpenTimelineIO, an EDL): those carry an edit between tools and leave out what frames must fix to make a result again, such as shaped glyphs, fitted tracks and pinned hashes. frames reads only what resolve wrote, so its form can follow frames.

A project file that keeps names and is resolved again on every render, as an NLE project relinks media and lays out titles with the fonts it finds: the result would change with the machine's fonts, packs, providers and theme, and the principle of a result reproducible from one file would not hold.

Pinned media named by the kind `probe` reports, `video`, `image` or `audio`, in place of `file`: the three are one reference, a file and its hash, whose frame times say whether it plays, so three kinds would hold one form, and a reader would have to tell them apart by a fact the reference does not hold. `page` and `imageSequence` hold keys of their own, and `file` names what the reference holds.

Writing only the tracker's quadrilaterals and fitting the motion in evaluation: the fit is a decision like any other, and writing it leaves evaluation with arithmetic only.

Writing each frame's homography beside its quadrilateral: it is the closed form of the target's quadrilateral and the frame's, with nothing left to decide, so a written copy would be a second source evaluation would have to choose between.

A glyph written at the position where it is drawn, with its cluster's pen start written beside it: the placement offset that ties the two would be written nowhere, so a document whose pen start had been written as the drawn one would read as valid and report its characters' boxes elsewhere. A glyph written as its pen position and its offset holds the fact once, and a cluster's pen start is read from its first glyph.

A cluster's cursive scripts written as a list of codes: a list inside a cluster would set each cluster over many lines where every other cluster is one flat list of values, and codes separated by spaces are the form ScriptExtensions.txt itself writes a set of scripts in.

A cluster's `advance` left out, read as the distance from its pen start to the next one's less its run's `staticTracking`: the line's last character, and a space that hangs past its end, have no next pen start to read it from. Written beside the pens and the static tracking, the advance is a fact the reader checks against them.

A run's static tracking left unwritten, the distance between two pens split only by the `advance`: a document whose shaper folded the tracking into its advances would read as valid, and its characters' boxes would hold the tracking ([FRAMES-23](../FRAMES-23/SPEC.md#text-placement)).

A glyph run's turn and narrowing written as one matrix of its glyphs' space: a matrix holds any turn, skew and scale, where a run's glyphs are turned by one of two values and narrowed along one axis, so a document would hold forms resolve never writes. `sideways` and `horizontalScale` write the two facts resolve decides.

A group's glyphs written as a list of ranges, one in each run they lie in: a cluster would be a list inside a list where nearly every one needs one range, and a group's glyphs lie side by side as they are placed, so where they start and how many there are names them. A composition set whole in one face, the first that has all its characters: the shaping provider falls back for each grapheme cluster ([FRAMES-5](../FRAMES-5/SPEC.md#faces)), and the glyphs of a composition would no longer be the glyphs its characters are set in anywhere else.

A unit written as its glyphs, with evaluation finding which characters go with it: evaluation would apply the rule that gives each space a unit at every instant, where resolve decides it once, and the same membership would be written twice, in the unit's glyphs and in its characters' clusters. A unit written as its characters, and as the lines whose hanging spaces it draws, gives its glyphs from them.
